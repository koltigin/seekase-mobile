The product

A personal museum in your pocket.

  • Create named public cabinets across broad collecting categories.
  • Add photographs, category-specific details, stories, dates, makers, publishers, and provenance.
  • Discover collectors, follow, like, save, and comment.
  • Build progress through meaningful collection and streak badges.
03 / 08

Why Seeker

Identity and daily participation, with privacy.

  • Mobile Wallet Adapter and Sign In With Solana provide wallet-first entry.
  • Backend Mainnet verification produces a genuine Seeker Genesis badge.
  • A daily signed Memo check-in creates a visible collecting streak.
  • Wallet addresses and token details stay out of the public profile.

Derived verification

Profiles receive badge status. Raw wallet addresses, balances, token accounts, and signatures are excluded from public profile data.

Controlled access

Row Level Security, Edge Functions, private wallet identity tables, reporting, and blocking protect account boundaries.

User control

In-app account deletion removes owned online data and photos. Public blockchain transactions remain immutable.
