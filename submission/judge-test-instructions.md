# Seekase judge test instructions

## Install

1. Install `Seekase-Android-1.0.0.apk` on an Android device. A Solana Seeker is recommended for the complete wallet and SGT flow.
2. Open Seekase. The APK is standalone and does not require Metro or a development computer.

## Fast path

1. Review the three onboarding screens and continue to account entry.
2. Choose **Continue with Solana wallet** on Seeker, approve the connection and sign-in request, then return to Seekase.
3. Complete the collector profile if prompted.
4. Open **Discover**, browse a cabinet, and try Like, Save, Comment, Follow, and Ask Collector.
5. Open **Collections** to review account cabinets, or use **Add** to create a cabinet or object with a photo.
6. Open **Profile → Collector Identity** to inspect the private wallet verification state. Raw wallet addresses and token details are never placed on the public profile.
7. If the connected wallet genuinely holds an eligible Seeker Genesis Token, the backend-derived Seeker badge appears after Mainnet verification.
8. In Profile, use **Daily Check-in** to review the Mainnet Memo action. The wallet displays the transaction and network fee before explicit approval; Seekase charges no application fee.
9. Open **Settings → Support & recovery** for help, and Settings → Privacy for Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Community Guidelines.

## Alternative account entry

- Google and verified email entry are available for users without a compatible Solana wallet.
- Apple ID intentionally remains labeled **Coming Soon** because the Apple provider is not enabled.
- Wallet connection is optional for Google and email accounts. A compatible wallet can be linked later from the private profile settings.

## Safety notes

- Do not share seed phrases or private keys with Seekase.
- Wallet identity, SGT lookup details, token accounts, balances, and wallet addresses remain private.
- Public account collections are visible to other collectors. Device collections remain only on that phone.
- The app has no checkout, escrow, SOL transfer, token tipping, or marketplace purchase flow.
