Collect with care.

Seekase is built around curiosity, attribution, and respect for people and their collections.

RespectAuthenticitySafetyRightsReports

1. Respect collectors

Do not harass, threaten, shame, stalk, exploit, or target people based on protected characteristics. Disagreement about an object, attribution, or collection style must remain civil.

2. Describe objects honestly

Do not knowingly misrepresent authorship, edition, condition, provenance, rarity, ownership, verification, or affiliation. Clearly distinguish personal belief from documented fact. A Seekase badge verifies only the stated account or Seeker condition; it does not authenticate collection objects.

3. Keep content safe and lawful

Do not publish illegal goods or instructions, sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate content, graphic abuse, credible threats, violent extremist praise, malicious links, private personal data, or content intended to facilitate fraud. Regulated objects must follow applicable law and platform distribution rules.

4. Respect ownership and privacy

Upload only photographs and text you have the right to share. Credit creators and sources where appropriate. Do not expose another person’s address, contact details, financial information, wallet identity, or other sensitive data without valid permission.

5. No spam or manipulation

Do not use repetitive promotion, deceptive links, automated engagement, impersonation, coordinated manipulation, or misleading account networks. Seekase is a collector showcase, not a venue for unsolicited financial promotion.

6. Reporting and enforcement

Use the in-app report action for profiles, collections, items, comments, private messages, or conversations that may violate these rules. You can also block another collector, which prevents new messages in either direction. Reports are private to the reporting account and moderation system. Seekase may remove content or restrict accounts based on context, severity, history, and legal obligations.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Seekase reporting is not an emergency channel.
