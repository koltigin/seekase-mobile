How it works

Collect without limits.

Books, coins, stamps, miniature cars, cameras, art, vintage technology, everyday objects, and categories nobody has named yet. Seekase keeps the structure flexible and the experience simple.

01 — ORGANIZE

Build your cabinets

Create collections, add photographs and useful details, and keep each object connected to its story.

02 — SHOW

Open a personal museum

Present your collection through an image-led public profile designed around the objects themselves.

03 — CONNECT

Discover collectors

Follow people, appreciate collections, save references, leave comments, and continue private collector conversations.

Inside Seekase

A cabinet you can carry.

Browse living collections, shape a public collector profile, and keep the details that make each object worth remembering.

Seeker first

Proof without exposure.

Seekase treats your account, public collector profile, and wallet as separate identities. Verification can earn a badge without publishing the wallet behind it.

Seeker verified

Recognize genuine collectors

Eligible Seeker Genesis Token holders can receive a derived public badge after private Mainnet verification.

Wallet private

Keep the address out of the profile

Raw wallet addresses, token accounts, balances, and SGT mint details are not placed on public collector profiles.
